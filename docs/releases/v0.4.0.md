Sela shows the gaming line only while you play, recovers from click-through reliably, and opens on hover more smoothly.

- **Only while a game is playing** (System → Performance, on by default): the closed notch becomes the click-through FPS line only when the foreground application is rendering and covers its monitor. Two seconds after the game leaves the foreground, Sela returns to the normal notch. Browsers and video players are ignored; **Mark as game** covers windowed games.
- Fixed: with the gaming line closed, the overlay could stop drawing and ignore the tray menu and **Ctrl+Alt+N**, leaving Task Manager as the only way to quit. The tray and shortcut now always restore input, and **Quit Sela** always exits.
- Hover opens the notch after 150 ms and closes it 300 ms after the pointer leaves, even after clicking inside; **Pin** keeps it open. Softer springs and wider crossfades make opening and closing glide.

Download **Sela-0.4.0-setup-x64.exe** to install, **Sela-0.4.0-windows-x64.zip** for portable use, or **sela-notch.exe**. **SHA256SUMS.txt** contains download hashes. Builds are unsigned.
